How to read this page

Tools

WaitCheck

Finds s_waitcnt synchronization hazards by static analysis.

ConSan

Finds data races and concurrency issues while a kernel runs.

FpSan

Runs a reference and a candidate Triton kernel under FpSan and compares the digests of their outputs. Different digests mean the two kernels do not compute the same thing.

AddressSanitizer

Reports memory errors. These runs are committed by hand.

Tabs

Nightly gate

Unmatched fails the nightly. A matched fail on daily-consan-racy or daily-fpsan-mismatch is an expected control.

Workload survey

Observed only. A fail here does not change the nightly, and a missing scan is marked report missing.

FpSan

Open-source kernel pairs compared under FpSan. Observed only, so a digest mismatch here does not change the nightly.

Manual runs

Results the nightly image cannot produce yet. They do not move the Healthy pill.
